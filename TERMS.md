# Termin: Terms of Use

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Termin is a hackathon project (RevenueCat Shipaton 2026) and is provided as is, without warranties.

- **Termin Premium** is an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly). It renews automatically until you cancel it in your account settings. In this build, purchases run in RevenueCat's Test Store, and no real money is charged.
- **Word content** was generated with AI models, checked by a second model and reviewed by the developer. It can still contain mistakes; please report any you find.
- **Tutor feedback** is generated by an AI model and can be wrong. Use it as a helpful hint, not as a final judgment.
- Please do not misuse Tutor or the other AI features, for example by sending automated requests.
